# V105 PA-Support Study

This is one outcome-informed, reused-development NQ training-support ablation,
not a new official Apex rule, independent validation or operational promotion.
The fixed V102 Windows model, V63/V92 shadows and 48 sealed holdout price/outcome
dates remain unchanged. The design follows the conditional study in
`docs/research-pa-target-feasibility-20260912.md` and the authenticated population
audit. No genetic algorithm, additional grid, cutoff search or MNQ arm is added.

## Arms And Learning

A restores each original chronological V102 NQ model and account. B retains A's
forecasts but uses a PA-only stop <=140-tick nomination gate and the v104 nominal
stop <=5*target guard at the actual slipped entry. C is B except PA forecasts
come from the support-trained model. Evaluation is byte-identical in all arms;
the switch happens inside each account after its own next-date PA transition.
No final Windows fold is reused on earlier scoring dates.

All complete original event/label pairs and four-mode maturity cutoffs are
validated before excluding training stops above 140 ticks. Retained targets
are the original one-NQ net dollars, including genuine geometry-zero labels.
Date weights and the scaler are refitted on retained rows. Minimum support
remains 40 events, 20 dates and five distinct dates per tree leaf. Each fold uses
its original causal V102 setting, first-fold untuned anchor included. At most
three scalers and three four-head bundles (12 native fits) are allowed, with
42 ordered durable stages. Identical support requires exact source reuse; an
entirely identical population is redundant and must not launch a market trial.
No failed fit permits retry, fallback, another cutoff or weaker support.

## Admission And Execution

The old temporary Python installation lost some source files. V105 explicitly
readmits the immutable closed V102 archive, all 496 original fit receipts and
its full dependency closure, without pretending its old runner still executes.
Every one of the 13 current HGB/loss module bytes must equal the corresponding
original hash, with original Python/numpy/sklearn/scipy and platform versions.
New actual runtime paths are bound in the new lock. No old lock or temp path is
edited or recreated. All 126 original NQ envelopes are regenerated from the
three original models and compared exactly before any new fit or tape read.

The 617-date minute source ends 2026-06-12; reconstructed event hashes must equal
the original broad-source hash. Training populations must match the earlier
audit. The original 181 explicit same-maturity raw pairs, receipt hashes and
window hashes must match the V102 lock. The 126 scoring dates are three original
42-date folds, with ten full-calendar purge sessions. Every raw pair is scanned
even if an account has already terminated. NQ's full four-mode unit journals
are recomputed from raw first-touch and adverse paths. Neither synthetic tests
nor archived labels alone establish raw execution conformance.

The original one-NQ cap, half-headroom allocator, costs, clocks, targets,
intratrade drawdown/MAE, PA start, rolling activity and payout machinery remain.
Support exclusions do not consume cooldown; actual-entry guard skips consume
the original resolution-plus-five-minute cooldown. Twelve own-state accounts
run once per scored date, 1,512 scheduled account-day slots, not independent
observations. A must reproduce all four full source accounts; B/C must reproduce
all eight Evaluation prefixes. Nomination, guard, capacity and busy skips remain
distinguishable from terminal tails and from fills.

## Decision And Publication

C-B estimates the joint training-support/weights/scaler effect. B-A is the
bundled nomination-and-entry-gate effect, not an isolated guard experiment.
Learning benefit requires positive C-B PA trading PnL in BOTH baseline and
combined stress, without added hard/MAE breaches or earlier inactivity closure.
Closure uses the first failing inclusive 30-calendar-day PA activity window,
including nontrading gaps. Cost-only and latency-only remain reported diagnostics
and cannot replace either primary mode. A separate full-route screen requires
numeric Evaluation, PA survival, modeled payout eligibility and zero hard/MAE
breaches in both primary modes. Official compliance and actual payout remain
unverified; program fees remain unpriced rather than fabricated.

The current V104 completion audit binds 13,168 prior comparisons. An exclusive
V105 run claim reserves two policies plus two contrasts, four charges to 13,172.
An owned technical abort still retains its reservation and fit-stage receipts;
it is not a profitability verdict. No second run is allowed after a claim.
Outcomes remain in memory until all training, all raw dates, exact controls,
account summaries and postrun integrity checks finish. Publication is immutable
create-if-absent. There is no partial-metric output, early-PnL stopping or live
deployment. `--preflight` and `--freeze` perform zero new fits or account replays.

Implementation test status, actual freeze/claim status and terminal market
evidence are separate facts; this design document alone proves none of them.
