# V143 Complete Population and Fresh Replay Components

October 12, 2026 UTC. Mac research only. These components complete two missing
parts of the existing continuation-model path. They do not define a new model,

## Why These Components

The bounded collector can finish successfully while still holding only a
prefix. A fitting caller needs a separate complete-population boundary, not
permission inferred from a successful batch or from NQ support alone. The
student also changes which trades occur, so evaluation must start four fresh
accounts and query both teacher and student at each account's own state. A
teacher's saved account trajectory is not the student's evaluation trajectory.

## Complete Population Reader

tools/nq_apex_continuation_complete_v143.py requires an externally accepted
central-state digest and exactly 256 completed cells before opening labels.
It retains the original source, first-fold teacher, numeric runtime, accelerated
review, qualification, checkpoint and all external terminal checks. New caller
dependencies are explicit and disjoint; they cannot replace the original 250
or accelerated 13 pins or expand the original collector's authority.

The entire immutable inventory is closed and regular. Original cell semantics,
ordered hash chaining, eight separate roll-in states and complete ten-session
contexts are checked before the unchanged assembler returns both product
populations. Both products must meet every original mode's support requirement
before any fit. Source, cursor, dependencies and inventory are rechecked after
assembly. There is no partial-product return, fitting, publication or admission
decision in this helper.

## Fresh Account Replay

tools/nq_apex_continuation_replay_v143.py consumes exactly the fixed 75 scoring
dates, February 23 through June 12, 2026, with four new accounts. It consumes
empty dates and terminal-account tails too. Missing, extra or reordered days,
different-maturity product pairs and differing original event censuses fail.
Frames are loaded one day at a time; their generator stays open for all four
books on that date and closes on success or failure.

The native student adapter combines market15 with the SAME account state,
phase/day counters and teacher raw8 to obtain the original 31 features. It uses
the fixed first-fold teacher cutoff and post-embargo student cutoff, preserves
all four raw advantages, and does not clip, invert, reselect or refit them.
Identical source-window or predictor inputs must remain deterministic across
books. Callback mutation of state values, type or additional attributes fails.

After all dates, the original economics checks audit account arithmetic,
counter continuity, Evaluation-to-PA transitions and gate inputs. A separate
saved-state pass recomputes every raw8/raw4 output at its recorded own state and
compares canonical values. Known-zero-capacity decisions preserve nulls and
cannot invoke either predictor. No partial metrics are returned or published.

## Authority Limits

These are components, not the post-collection stage runner. Caller-supplied
hashes and native metadata are not source or model-artifact authentication.
The later runner must freeze its expanded dependency closure and qualification,
require external complete256 acceptance, restore authenticated teachers, use
the native fit journal, and restore both authenticated student artifacts before
replay. Saved predictions must also be audited with restored model bytes.

The helper flags therefore deliberately leave source admission, fitting
authority, market execution, independent validation, current official account
compliance and orders false. Invented-tick software tests are not strategy
evidence. Historical development results do not become independent validation.
The sealed June 29-September 2 holdout stays closed. No new counted comparison.
