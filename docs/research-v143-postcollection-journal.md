## Why This Component

The collector deliberately ends with zero student fits. Reusing the V137 fit
journal unchanged would miscount the new learner: V137 expects six pipelines
with ten native fits each, whereas this continuation learner needs two product
pipelines with twelve native fits each. Its existing small in-memory model
round-trip test also cannot prove authenticated production artifact restoration.

The new tools/nq_apex_continuation_journal_v143.py reuses the V138 durable
publication and regular-file helpers, not its model schema. No existing frozen
source, model, collector, scientific protocol or market evidence was edited.

## Implemented Boundary

Journal.start/native_observe/persist/finish/fail/complete record NQ then MNQ.
Each product preserves four modes, each ordered as x scaler, y scaler, HGB.
The result has 24 native fits, 48 native start/completion events and four
pipeline events. Actual UTC execution times are separate from simulated model
information cutoffs. Source, plan, population and numeric-runtime hashes are
caller-bound. Partial, failed, conflicting or modified evidence cannot resume.

The helper persists complete models and authenticates their exact byte count
and SHA-256 before pickle load. It checks fixed HGB parameters/100 iterations,
product/feature/head order and twelve distinct estimator/scaler objects per
product, including scaler widths 31 and 1 and native scaler metadata.

Exact raw4 prediction parity covers every row of all four mode inputs in
bounded chunks, not a few baseline rows. Input and prediction fingerprints are
independent of chunk size. restore_model repeats the saved fingerprints without
fitting; verify_journal audits the complete closed 55-file event/artifact record
without loading a pickle or predicting. Canonical JSON rejects duplicate keys,
BOMs and trailing noncanonical bytes even when a descriptor is resealed.

Identity hashes are not source admission. The caller must first authenticate
the source and qualify BOTH complete product populations before ANY fit. This
helper does not assemble or authorize a population, restore market teachers,
execute account replay, protect against a malicious local administrator, or
establish model profitability. Its manifest explicitly leaves those claims false.
