## Stateful Inner Objective

For each product, outer fold, setting, inner block and mode, start a fresh
unchanged Legacy50K Evaluation account: balance/peak50000USD, floor47500USD,
no carried activity or pending transition. Process every scheduled date,
including dates with no selected events. Carry state across the five dates,
but not between the two inner blocks. Never reset daily or erase losses after
a modeled reset. Use the original raw-tick account engine, including overlapping
intents, actual integer quantity, skipped geometry, cooldown, stop/target,
commissions, slippage, trailing threshold, and lifecycle processing. Unit-event
counterfactual labels cannot be summed as a substitute for account PnL.

For each setting, sum the two blocks' recorded net trading PnL separately for
baseline, cost-only, latency-only and combined stress. Its score is the minimum
of these four totals, in exact integer cents. Choose the largest score among
settings with no hard-threshold breach or MAE violation in any of the eight
inner episodes. Keep every episode, including failures. Missing reports are
errors, not zero profit. No supported safe setting means no selection and a
terminal failure, not a profitable fallback. A negative best score stays
negative; zero-trade zero is admissible but is not a pass. Exact ties use the
original anchor-first grid index. Do not choose modes or introduce epsilon ties.

This objective is min(mode, sum(block, net)), not sum(block, min(mode, net)).
A strong block may offset a weak one; this does not establish both-block
profitability. Program fees remain unpriced and their units are reported
separately. Trading commissions/slippage are already deducted once. Five fresh
dates cannot meet the seven active Evaluation-day requirement, enter PA, or
establish PA inactivity survival. These short episodes choose hyperparameters,
not account certification. Do not add ad hoc drawdown weights or a new trading
threshold after observing the results.
