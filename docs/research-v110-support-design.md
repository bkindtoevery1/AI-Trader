## Pre-observation Decision

Before opening the saved prediction arrays for this screen, choose these
heuristic informational requirements: disagreement on at least10 of the126
original scored development dates, and at least one differing date in each
original chronological42-date fold. This is not statistical power, independent
replication, a PA-trade minimum or a profitability gate. Its purpose is to avoid
another V108-style expensive comparison driven by a single new nomination.
Thresholds cannot be lowered after seeing this screen. No dates or model modes
may be selected to improve its result. Sparse or identical support defers the
performance experiment without asserting that the strategy would lose money.

Compare both products over every original same-event row. Validate complete
ordered envelope coverage, native product reference exposure, exact event/time/
feature/stop identity, finite original four-mode predictions and stored selection.
All-four-strictly-positive is unchanged; None/zero/negative remain abstentions.
Apply the unchanged PA stop<=140 mask in both arms. Malformed rows outside that
mask must still fail. Count NQ-only and MNQ-only changes, not only additions.

Only pinned, already-completed V102 saved forecasts are used. No fresh market
prices, raw tapes, labels, model fits, predictions, account paths or PA outcomes
are evaluated. The saved status contains old complete development outcomes, but
the screen does not access or return those fields. This is not full causal-model
restoration or source readmission; those remain necessary before an account replay.

A support pass does not prove an actual PA disagreement: actual phase dates,
busy/cooldown state, account capacity, positive net target and the nominal guard
can still remove all hypothetical differences. A later replay must establish its
own real exposures, retain exact V107 controls and identical Evaluation prefixes,
and preserve every original cash, cost, risk, inactivity and payout rule.
