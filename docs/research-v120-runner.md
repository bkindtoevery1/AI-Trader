# V120 Execution Boundary

The design remains `research-v120-design.md`; this runner adds no treatment,
threshold, learner or fallback. It connects the already implemented raw-pressure
admission, two-arm causal pipeline and eight new account books to the exact
closed V119 controls. The operational Windows models are not changed.

The full raw trade-size scan previously completed without fitting. The runner
pins that complete report and its observed exit0 process receipt, checks every
recorded dependency, restores the original source and causal populations, and
matches all181 raw scan receipts,5485 contexts and training bindings. A
self-consistent new context hash is not admitted. The account replay still
reads all181 raw pairs; the cached admission avoids a redundant volume scan.

Focused and full regression processes need actual terminal exit codes, unchanged
source/runtime bindings and a complete unique test-case census. The full suite
may retain only the exact two previously documented unrelated failures; this is
not a green-suite claim. Every current focused case must also exist in the full
suite. A test receipt from an earlier component revision cannot qualify this
runner. There is no market fit in qualification.

The selected child interpreter is probed separately before and after execution;
the parent's runtime cannot attest the different full-test environment. Its
interpreter, standard-library/package code and metadata inventory are bound by
the versioned probe. This is filesystem/runtime reproducibility evidence, not
attestation of the entire OS or transient loaded memory.

Execution uses a new immutable lock and exclusive claim. Only that claim reserves
four comparisons,13209 to13213. Existing claims, failures or other output stop
reruns, including after a crash. Six forests require18 ordered, durable stage
receipts and six exact population-bound exports. Neither a failed observer nor
a missing export may advance to the next fit. All126 scored dates and both
pressure arms must be present before the complete account replay. All eight
retained control books are byte-compared against the pinned predecessor.
Each date's distribution must additionally match its own arm/fold's durable
model identity, population binding and exact target table; self-hashes alone
cannot admit swapped candidate predictions.

No partial economic result is printed. After the full replay, the runner checks
the unchanged Evaluation prefixes, distributions, input/runtime hashes,
recorded-account arithmetic and immutable model receipts before publishing a
terminal result and seal. Failure produces technical-attribution evidence,
never an inferred strategy failure or an automatic rescue run. The arithmetic
auditor does not independently reconstruct raw first crossings or official
Apex/personal-account eligibility.

The separate observed postrun child rechecks the exact output census, original
calendar/control bytes, eighteen ordered stage records, six sealed model exports,
arithmetic and the complete economic summary. It does not refit or replay raw
markets. A completion packet requires actual exit0 for both market and postrun
children, the postrun observer's exact summary hash, unchanged qualified sources
and all immutable outputs. That packet says completed development, not success.
The market observer pins the result seal. The postrun manifest retains hashes
of the bytes actually validated and rejects files changed during audit; it never
re-hashes a replacement into an apparently valid completion manifest.

Current CLI sequence: `--check focused --key focused-v2`, `--check full --key full-v1`,
`--qualify --focused focused-v2 --full full-v1`, `--run`, `--verify`, `--complete`.
The retained focused-v1 preceded the four integrity repairs and is not reused
to qualify their final bytes. The selected-runtime probe includes the actual
macOS Python framework binary, not just its launcher. Its package inventory
does not read raw market or sealed holdout directories.
Each check/run retains an exclusive process intent, started receipt, output and
terminal receipt. If a handle is live, wait for it; never dispatch again merely
because observation timed out. A nonzero exit is retained as a failure.

This is reused, outcome-informed historical development, not independent
validation or a confirmed50K pass. Program fees and personal account cohort
remain unverified. Sealed holdout dates stay closed, orders stay disabled,
and Windows readiness is not a prerequisite.
