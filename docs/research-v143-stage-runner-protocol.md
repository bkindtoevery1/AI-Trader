# V143 One-Shot Postcollection Runner Protocol

Freeze this execution contract, the explicit implementation/test inventory and
successful software qualification before the first actual student fit. This
continues the already charged V143 candidate; it does not change its scientific
design or supply another model trial. Mac research only. Windows, signals,
orders, schedules, purchases and the sealed holdout remain outside scope.

## Admission And Hash Direction

Only an independently observed, fully audited256-cell checkpoint can enter
the stage. A partial cursor must reject before reading a source factory,
labels, authentication receipts or creating an output directory. Every original
cell, source dependency, teacher artifact and external successful terminal
remains mandatory. An empty/stale/missing/in-flight/failed input is not accepted.

The CLI requires --run-reviewed-stage, --current-state-sha256 and
--admission-sha256. Both digests must come from the parent-reviewed evidence,
not from the child computing a current hash to approve itself. The central
v143_postcollection_stage_v1 record pins the fixed execution-admission receipt.
That receipt binds the accepted cursor, complete artifact inventory, original
claim, collector authority, source/teacher/plan/runtime, full code inventory,
qualification, source and teacher authentication references, output and limits.
It omits the full central-state digest to avoid a circular hash dependency.
The parent computes the trusted central digest only after its admission record
is published. This is evidence handling, not a new user-approval requirement.

Inputs use closed native JSON, unique keys, exact types, canonical bytes and
SHA256 bindings. Regular unlinked files and nonlinked paths are required.
The source authentication binds all181 original source dates and raw/window
receipts and the exact blocked application-file census. Teacher authentication
binds the fixed first-fold artifacts and bindings. These receipts attest to the
existing historical current-content restoration route, not renewed independent
provider provenance, a fresh holdout or current official account compliance.
Original source restoration and model authentication execute again; receipts
alone are never substituted for checking the underlying content.

The successful synthetic qualification must bind the exact283-file inventory:
original250, accelerated13, qualified component17 and runner/test/protocol3.
The sets are disjoint. No original or qualified component source may change
to fit the new runner. Source/teacher/qualification records precede execution
review. Missing qualification or authentication never causes auto-generation.
No actual admission receipt is authorized while the population remains partial.

Keep the original32 anchors,41-date ten-inclusive-session label calendar,
10 ignored post-label embargo sessions and75 scoring dates. Both product/four-
mode populations must qualify before the first scaler or estimator is fit.
Never fit NQ first as a fallback for insufficient MNQ support. The exact fixed

four HGB heads per product, parameters, cutoffs and all-row artifact parity.
No pairing, shortened horizon, selected labels, refit, inversion or reselection.

Use only the original source loader and original one-day tape adapter. Fresh
teachers and restored students drive four fresh accounts for all75 dates.
Preserve long/short, sizing, fees, slippage, account guards and Evaluation-to-PA
survival/payout/breach/inactivity semantics. A second fresh-model audit checks
all serialized own-state teacher/raw8 and student/raw4 outputs. All four modes
are mandatory; publish no partial economic results or selection feedback.

Private function clones may extend the qualified stage's monitor scopes with
the authenticated blocked-file list and wrap native fit observers with resource
checks. They may not patch frozen modules, callable identities, monitor owners,
scientific parameters or source globals. The original complete-reader and stage
integrity checks continue to run. External receipts and central acceptance are
reauthenticated at phase boundaries and after source restoration, not only at
the very end. Tape receipts are checked after their context has closed too.

## Resources And Irreversibility

The actual stage uses an8GiB process high-water bound and a14,400-second wall
budget declared before observation. Checks occur at phase, native-observer,
product-fit and tape open/close boundaries. These are cooperative limits, not
a hard OS allocation sandbox or an interruption timer; a native call can exceed
the limit before returning. An exceedance invalidates the attempt, never a
financial result or reason to silently enlarge the budget. No new workers,
timer signal owners or network paths are added by this wrapper.

The fixed root is reports/nq_apex_continuation_stage_v143. It must not exist
before execution. The wrapper claims it before source restoration/assembly,
and the frozen stage writes into its exclusive stage child. Failed, partial or
interrupted roots remain preserved; no overwrite, rename-and-retry, alternate
output path, second model fit or automatic repair. Qualification does not start
the actual run. Any genuine failure requires attribution before a separately
reviewed remedy; this protocol provides no automatic rerun authority.

Attempt/admission scope counts describe the fixed expected calculation, not
observed fits or account completions. Actual completed counts may be concluded
only from the full authenticated child journal and replay plus successful
wrapper termination. Stdout and wrapper completion contain metadata and hashes,
not balances, targets or partial metrics. A wrapper failure invalidates even a
previously written child completion for conclusions.

## Completion Evidence

After the frozen full stage, recheck source, teachers, complete cell inventory,
all external receipts, exact central digest, resource limits and the closed
output inventory. Record actual fit/artifact/replay evidence and real UTC time;
never backdate to the simulated training cutoff. Success cannot publish its
own external process-exit receipt or advance research promotion.

The parent must observe the actual command exit, verify every saved hash, and
independently audit the serialized account arithmetic, lifecycle and own-state
predictions before reading a complete economic conclusion. A completion marker,
green synthetic tests or absence of an exception alone is not completion.
The original baseline-and-stress full-route gates remain unchanged. Unfavorable
outcomes stay failures; no tuning from these scores. Keep total comparisons
13,300, the48 sealed June29-September2 dates closed, and official compliance and
independent-validation claims false. The current overall goal remains active.
