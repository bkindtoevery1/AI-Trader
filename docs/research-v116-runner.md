# V116 Economic Runner

This implements the already declared cross-product score-stacking comparison.
The fitted-model design, source population, cost assumptions and economic gates
are unchanged. Implementation alone is not a claimed or fitted market trial.

## Source And Prediction Binding

The runner verifies the completed V115 closure and additive erratum, the exact
V113 control closure and the V116 component/source qualification. Original
source admission restores all six original product/fold models. The unchanged
V112/V113 population construction supplies date-equal mature original labels;
the V116 adapter adds original prior-OOS NQ scores and their availability mask.
Reconstructed input identities must match the actual component admission.

Saved V113 monotone coefficients and all 126 saved forecast envelopes are
restored without refitting. The two new calibrators use only earlier original
outer-fold forecasts, not current-model backpredictions or candidate returns.
Every current NQ score is paired with the same MNQ event and decision clock.
The source, plan and both model vintages remain bound. Fold one passes through
the original MNQ forecasts exactly; only folds two and three may be fitted.

## Replay Contract

Three arms use the frozen account/execution kernel across 181 explicit raw
NQ/MNQ pairs and 126 scored dates. All arms retain original NQ Evaluation;
only each account's subsequent next-date MNQ PA forecasts differ. There are
12 continuous books and 1,512 scheduled account-days, including zero tails.

The saved V113 account name `monotone_confidence_MNQ_PA` maps explicitly to the
V116 policy name `monotone_MNQ_PA`. This is an outer comparison-label alias,
not an edit to the saved account payload. The saved original and monotone
accounts must reproduce exactly in all four cost/latency modes. The four new
candidate Evaluation prefixes must equal the original Evaluation prefixes.
All own-product labels and raw-source/window receipts are reconciled too.

Both stack-minus-original and stack-minus-monotone comparisons must improve
PA net in baseline AND combined stress without added hard/MAE breaches or
earlier inactivity. Positive absolute PA net, observed survival, modeled
full-route success and hypothetical payout eligibility are distinct results.
Lower calibration error, cost-only or latency-only gains cannot rescue failure.
There is no program-fee-inclusive profit or verified personal account claim.

## Qualification And Execution

The runner requires observed collection, focused tests, no-fit actual-source
admission and full regression processes. It verifies exact scoped test inventory,
commands, child PIDs/exits, source/runtime fingerprints, raw logs and JUnit.
All new tests must pass. The two exact preexisting full-suite failures remain
visible and are reconciled against the frozen component baseline; they do not
make the full suite green or exempt any new failure, missing case or skip.
An independent final source-bound review must have no unresolved findings.

Only then may an immutable preoutcome lock and exclusive claim be published.
The claim charges three comparisons once, changing the ledger from 13,196 to
13,199. Twenty durable fit-stage receipts cover two transforms, eight scalar
fits and two model completions. At most 24 coefficients are learned; no original
HGB or original feature scaler is refitted. The lock records the exact source,
both product-vintage plans, original control identities, runtime and code.

The runner publishes no candidate metrics during fitting or partial replay.
Only completed pair counts are emitted. After complete replay it verifies the
entire bound state again, then publishes the result and seal create-if-absent.
Post-claim errors preserve a technical-abort record and the comparison charge;
no automatic retry, new optimizer, parameter search or uncounted successor.
The process observer must retain the real child exit and reap a child if its
receipt publication fails. A file or timeout alone is not completion evidence.

Admission is available through `--audit-inputs`; it cannot fit or claim.
`--preflight` additionally requires full runner qualification but does not
create a claim. `--freeze-and-run` is a one-attempt research action, never a
live worker or a recurring task. Complete results still require independent
post-run source restoration and recorded-account arithmetic verification.

The 48 historical holdout dates remain sealed. Historical adaptive reuse is
development evidence only. This research runner never changes an operational
model, Windows process, Telegram, credential, order, spending limit, schedule
or paused heartbeat. The separately user-authorized Windows local-paper
restoration is an operational task, not V116 qualification or deployment.
