## Failure Attribution

All six searches chose lower internal mean-squared dollar error than the
original anchor. Five selected L2=100; the remaining MNQfold2 selected L2=1.
The chosen learning rates were NQ0.025/0.025/0.1 and MNQ0.025/0.025/0.05.
NQ selected events were0/0/119 versus original8/3/112. MNQ selected events
were0/0/0 versus original0/0/95. Events are forecasts, not executed fills.

The observed improvement in aggregate inner prediction loss did not translate
to positive executable utility. Suppressing early NQ opportunities and every
MNQ opportunity describes the failure; it does not independently prove a
causal market mechanism or overfitting. Ten inner validation dates per fold are
limited evidence, and all outer dates were previously observed development.
Correct nested selection does not retroactively make them an independent test.

Retain promising model families and the reusable chronological tuning code.
A future separately preregistered study may investigate a cost-aware trading
utility selection objective or another retained family. Do not rewritev100's
metric, select a profitable mode, fall back to an observed setting, open the
holdout or promote this model. No new successor is claimed by this report.
