# V140: Giveback-Aligned Unit Targets

Declared October7,2026 before new market labels or fits. This is a separate,
outcome-informed historical development study, not independent validation.
Components may be implemented and synthetically tested before reservation;
real labels, fits and account execution require complete qualification first.

## Question And Prior Failures

[V139](research-v139-results.md) failed its whole account route. It improved
NQ forecast error slightly, but did not improve baseline PA profit or stressed
Evaluation survival to a pass. Its original-exit unit labels differed from the
V136 giveback execution used by both arms. That known mismatch is a testable
hypothesis, not an established explanation or permission to rerun V139.

Replace ONLY the training response with guard-free, one-contract cash outcomes
under the unchanged V136 giveback exit. Preserve V139's18 exact feature floats,
learner, masks, folds, nomination and whole-account route. The sole primary
control is the exact completed V139 original-target forecasts and account books.
Using V134/V136 alone would also change features and confound this comparison.

Alignment itself is not new: V84 aligned changed stops; V109 aligned PA target
eligibility; V137 learned guarded giveback cash/floor transitions; V138 learned
their direct utility. This study differs by holding the paired unit learner
fixed and changing only guard-free unit exit targets. It does not claim to solve
account continuation value, dynamic headroom or the minimum-NQ capacity trap.

## Exact Unit Target

For each original event, product and each baseline/cost-only/latency-only/stress
mode, apply the original V87 geometry and entry clocks to the same admitted
explicit-contract raw-tick windows. Execute one actual contract, with original
commissions and adverse fills, through the unchanged V136 giveback kernel.
Use a fresh signal-only account per independent event, with no trailing-floor,
PA MAE or daily-loss guard. Never divide a guarded account outcome by quantity
and call it the unit label. Never change old V88 journals or source bindings.

The trade-local liquidation-net peak arms at the original conservative reserve
R, then exits at the first tick where twice liquidation net is at most the peak.
The reserve is NOT just stop distance times tick value. Preserve stop, target,
giveback and deadline precedence, provider sequence for equal timestamps, the
first timely deadline tick, and actual gap losses. A target exit can prevent
giveback from ever changing a label. Arming alone is not an effective treatment.

Under these fixed linear costs and quantity-independent fills, liquidation net,
peak and reserve all multiply by positive integer quantity, so the unguarded
crossing is quantity-invariant. This does NOT hold for account guards, capacity,
busy/cooldown, PA nominal-risk-ratio rejection, phase transitions or withdrawals.
Those remain in the actual account evaluation, outside this target definition.

Preserve original geometry skips as zero labels and all losing outcomes. Actual
account zero capacity must not become a unit zero. Each label receipt binds its
original event identity, mode, product, exact exit policy, complete window hash,
normal/delayed suffix identity, conservative full-window maturity and execution
evidence. An early giveback resolution must not shorten the training maturity
clock. Malformed, missing or out-of-order evidence aborts, never silently drops
an opportunity. A supplied-window hash is not provider provenance; authenticated
source restoration and whole-tape integrity remain the source adapter's duty.

## Required Qualification

Synthetic tests must cover both products and sides in all four modes; exact
arming/half-peak boundaries; never-armed and target-before-arm paths; gap losses;
same-tick ordering, equal timestamps and suffix invariance; missing or malformed
complete windows; fresh per-event peaks; MNQ quantity1..6 cancellation; and
guarded counterexamples. Independent arithmetic must check crossing outcomes.

Source integration must separately authenticate new labels, old journals,
original populations, complete windows and the sealed control. It must prove
feature/mask/weight identity, prefix maturity and immutable publication order.
The existing V139 adapter checks old labels; bypassing it or replacing its y
while claiming unchanged label provenance is forbidden. Component tests alone
do not qualify a market experiment or establish profitable behavior.

No Windows change, live model, Telegram message, schedule, order, purchase,
genetic algorithm or sealed holdout access belongs to this study. Historical
development success, if any, still does not prove an actual50K account pass.
