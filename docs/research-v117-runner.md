# V117 Economic Runner

The unchanged qualified V117 account adapter is now connected to an explicit
two-arm, four-mode historical experiment. The hypothesis and economics remain
exactly those of `docs/research-v117-design.md`; this adds execution and evidence,
not new model fitting, selection, thresholds or data collection.

The runner restores all original causal V102 fitted records, scalers, training
populations, settings, selection snapshots and complete forecast envelopes via
the original V107 admission. Its exact 181-date projection must match the
completed V107 comparator and V117 component admission. Latest completed V116,
its immutable outputs, and the component qualification are hash-bound. Source
inspection forbids candidate nomination/replay. No raw evaluation or current PA
labels are used to alter the nomination rule.

Before freezing or claiming, require independent exact scoped test collection,
all focused cases passing, source restoration with zero new candidate work,
the complete regression with only exact disclosed preexisting failures, and a
source-bound review. JUnit identity mapping and the inherited narrow ZIP fixture
equivalence are verified. Real process PIDs, terminal exits, byte hashes, runtime
identity and collection-before-tests evidence are required, not success labels.

One create-if-absent claim reserves two comparisons (13,199 to 13,201). No fits
or fit receipts exist. Run eight independent continuous accounts, reverify every
raw own-product unit journal across 181 paired sessions, and compare four full
control reports and four candidate Evaluation projections exactly. Nomination is
shared across modes but actual prices, costs, guards, quantities, cooldown and
account states are mode-specific. Complete raw-pair counts are the only progress
output. Original forecasts remain unchanged and are retained in the final result.

Both primary modes must improve PA trading PnL without an added hard breach,
MAE violation or earlier inactivity. Absolute PA positivity, modeled full-route
success and hypothetical payout eligibility remain separate. No incomplete
calendar, favorable diagnostic, relative loss reduction or more trades can be
relabelled a verified account pass. Program fees and personal cohort limits stay
explicit; no claim of current official compliance or received payout is made.

Only a complete replay and reconciled terminal summary may be published. Lock,
claim, dependencies, qualified runtime, original input identity and comparator
bytes are checked again. Publication is immutable; conflicting or owned partial
publication yields a terminal technical abort, never an automatic restart or
performance verdict. The closed 48 historical holdout dates remain unopened.

After a successful market-process exit, a separate terminal audit must verify
source/forecast lineage and recorded-account arithmetic before interpretation.
This runner itself is not independent evidence of execution or statistical
validity. Reused historical outcomes remain development only. Windows, Telegram,
orders, credentials, purchases and schedules are not involved.
